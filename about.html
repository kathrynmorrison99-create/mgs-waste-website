Where we started

Originally, Morrison's Garbage Service Inc. provided residential garbage services for more than three decades. In 2000, we expanded our services to include dumpsters and demolition, eventually growing into what became known as MGS Waste.

Who we are today

In 2022, we sold the residential side of Morrison's Garbage Service Inc. and transitioned to MGS Waste LLC, allowing us to focus solely on our specialty services: dumpsters, demolition, and waste removal. Today, MGS Waste LLC remains a family-owned and family-operated business backed by a team of seasoned professionals. We proudly serve Rowan County and most surrounding counties, bringing decades of experience and dependable service to every job.

Why it matters

We take pride in using top-of-the-line, up-to-date equipment to help ensure our work is completed safely, efficiently, and professionally. MGS Waste LLC is also fully insured, giving our customers confidence that their projects are in trusted and capable hands.

From day one, our goal has been simple: provide dependable service, quality work, and a level of care our customers can count on.
