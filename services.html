FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

What kinds of demolition does MGS Waste handle?
  • Residential (houses, additions, garages, mobile homes, fire-damaged)
  • Barns, sheds, and outbuildings
  • Commercial (retail, office, light industrial)
  • Asbestos testing coordination
  • Post-demolition cleanup
How does the demolition process work?
  1. Free on-site quote within 1–2 days
  2. Permits and prep, including asbestos testing coordination
  3. Demolition
  4. Hauling and cleanup with our own trucks and dumpsters
How much does it cost to demolish a house in North Carolina?

Cost depends on the size of the structure, the materials involved, whether asbestos is present, and how accessible the site is. We provide a free on-site quote so you know the cost upfront.

What can I not put in a dumpster?
  • Automotive tires
  • Automotive batteries
  • Friable asbestos
What's included with the compactor?
  • Equipment supply, or service of your existing unit
  • Scheduled and on-call pickups
  • Full maintenance
  • One local number for everything

We also service personal roll-off dumpsters for a simpler setup.

Is a compactor right for you?

Compactors work well for manufacturers, distribution centers, grocery and retail locations, and property managers — anywhere that generates a steady, high volume of waste.

Can I keep a dumpster beyond 30 days?

Yes — additional rental fees apply if you need the container longer than the standard 30-day rental period.
